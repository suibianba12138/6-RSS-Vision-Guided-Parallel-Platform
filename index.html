Scope, limitations & media provenance ＋

This is a source-backed engineering showcase for an experimental prototype. The displayed robot is a conceptual vector schematic, not a CAD-accurate model, real photo, animation capture or physical test. The interactive explorer is an explanatory illustration, not live camera data or a running controller. No accuracy, timing, stability, closed-loop or safety performance is claimed. The native inverse-solver DLL’s redistribution terms remain unverified; original controller scripts include historical variants and require hardware-specific review before operation. The future demonstration will use authentic, reviewed footage.
